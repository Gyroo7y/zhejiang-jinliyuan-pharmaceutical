## Reference translation

- Novartis: adopted its comfortable whitespace, humane lead image and modular editorial rhythm, without copying layout or messaging.
- GSK: adopted a balance between human warmth and pharmaceutical professionalism, with less overt consumer/patient framing.
- Samsung Biologics: adopted the evidence-led `Why` chapter, metric restraint and clear transition into manufacturing/quality.
- Domestic pharmaceutical patterns: products use category plus search; formal public documents remain in Information Disclosure rather than becoming a marketing-led main story.

## Remaining limitations

1. Generated still images plus CSS motion cannot substitute for approved factory photography, packaging cut-outs or a professional hero film.
2. English interface is complete, but formal source bodies without approved translations remain Chinese to avoid invention.
3. Unavailable legacy PDF sources remain pending retrieval; no substitute document was fabricated.
4. The local hash-route prototype is appropriate for review, but production should use deployable semantic routes and a validated CMS/content workflow.
5. Contact form is deliberately demonstrative: it sends and stores nothing.

## Acceptance statement

V1 is accepted for Owner visual and content review. It must not be described as deployed, published, legally approved, medically reviewed, or ready to replace the live website.
